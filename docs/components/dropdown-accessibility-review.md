# Dropdown manual accessibility review

Status: Pending. Automated axe checks do not establish complete accessibility.

Record tester, date, OS, browser, assistive technology, steps, results and defects for:

- Native trigger naming, expanded state and popup relationships.
- Menu labels, disabled actions, typeahead, first/last opening and recursive submenu navigation.
- Top-only Escape, Tab/Shift+Tab, outside interaction and focus restoration after consumer actions.
- Arbitrary forms, labels, validation and normal tab order without a menu role or focus trap.
- Hover pointer travel, touch activation and manual/controlled modes.
- RTL/localized text, long labels, zoom/reflow, light/dark contrast, forced colors and reduced motion.
- Screen-reader announcement and reading order across portaled menus.

No human pass, device pass or stable maturity is claimed until these records exist.
